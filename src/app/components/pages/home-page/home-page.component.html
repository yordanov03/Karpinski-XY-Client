Don’t Change Reality, Create It

My reflections are not singular in nature, nor are they an attempt to give a solution. Instead, they are a declaration of existence and a materialization of a state that, among its participants, is an everyday, unnoticed naturalness.

About Me

A corporate by day

My name is Pawel Karpinski, I was born in Radom, Poland, and grew up in Tarnobrzeg. I moved to Wroclaw in 2006 to study finance. After earning my degree from Wroclaw University of Economics in 2011, I continued to expand my education and artistic horizons. In 2019, I graduated from the Eugeniusz Geppert Academy of Fine Arts and Design in Wroclaw, where I studied painting under the tutelage of Prof. Piotr Błażejewski and Dr. Anna Kołodziejczyk.

I moved to Zurich in 2022 and since then my work focuses on observing and understanding how Swiss society works and how it interacts with nature. As an immigrant, I can see things from the inside, but as an outsider, I have to come to my own conclusions.
