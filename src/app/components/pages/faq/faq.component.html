FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

  • An artist by night?

    I have a 9-5 job alongside my artistic practice. I graduated from Wroclaw University of Economics and have been working in the finance industry ever since.

  • Who is your favourite artist?

    Edward Stachura, Piet Mondrian, Edward Hopper, and Franz Kline.

  • What is your favourite music?

    Bjork and Katarzyna Nosowska are my absolute favourites. During painting sessions, I listen to Massive Attack and artists from the Ninja Tune label, particularly Skalpel, Cinematic Orchestra, Bonobo, and Nicolas Jaar.

  • How do you come up with ideas for the paintings?

    I look for obscure contrasts where people leave their marks. For more details, see my artist statement on the About Me page.

  • Do you sell your paintings?

    Yes, you can find available works in the Paintings section. If interested, please make an inquiry via this website or send me a direct message on social media.

  • Do you ship worldwide?

    Yes, paintings are prepared for secure travel in a wooden frame. Contact me directly to discuss shipping options and costs.

  • Where can I see your works?

    I am working on my exhibition in Zurich—more info coming soon! In the meantime, you can view my works in person at my atelier in Zurich.

  • Do you paint commissions?

    I prefer to choose my own subjects but am flexible to a certain extent.

  • Do you paint only abstract art?

    I believe abstract art is universal and provides space for open interpretation. While I focus on basic elements of visual language, I occasionally practice figurative art.
