FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

  • An artist by night?

    I have a 9-5 job alongside my artistic practice. I have first graduated from the Wroclaw University of Economics and I am working in finance industry ever since.

  • Who is your favourite artist?

    Edward Stachura, Piet Mondrian, Edward Hopper, Franz Kline.

  • What is your favourite music?

    Bjork and Katarzyna Nosowska are my absolute favourites. During a painting session I listen to Massive Attack and artists working with Ninja Tune label, in particular Skalpel, Cinematic Orchestra, Bonobo and Nicolas Jaar.

  • How do you come up with ideas for the paintings?

    I look for obscure contrasts, where people leave their marks. For more details see my artist statement in About Me page.

  • Do you sell your paintings?

    Yes, you can find available works in the Paintings section. Should you be interested, please make an enquiry via this website or send me a direct message on social media.

  • Do you ship worldwide?

    Yes, paintings will be prepared for secure travel in a wooden frame. Contact me directly to discuss shipping options and cost.

  • Where can I see your works?

    I am working on my exhibition in Zurich, more info soon! In the meantime you can view my works in person in my atelier in Zurich.

  • Do you paint commissions?

    I prefer to chose my own subject, but I am flexible to certain extent.

  • Do you paint only abstract art?

    I believe that abstract art is a lot more universal and gives a large space for open interpretation. With a little effort it can be both entertaining and rewarding for the viewer. I focus my practice on basic elements of visual language and complex, figurative references with all their contexts would only divert the attention form the point. I am practicing figurative art from time to time, though.
