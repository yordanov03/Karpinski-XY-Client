About Me

Where I Come From

I was born in Radom, Poland, and grew up in Tarnobrzeg. After earning my degree from Wroclaw University of Economics in 2011, I continued to expand my education and artistic horizons. In 2019, I graduated from the Eugeniusz Geppert Academy of Fine Arts and Design in Wroclaw, where I studied painting under the tutelage of Prof. Piotr Błażejewski and Dr. Anna Kołodziejczyk.

Statement

I chose visual arts and painting specifically because I am fascinated by visual language and its flexibility. Over the years, I developed an abstract style that balances organic forms and geometry. Inspired by architecture and human interaction, my work explores intimacy between people and their environments. This interaction leaves a subconscious trace in our routines, moods, and how we spend time.

I see painting as a message that can be interpreted differently depending on the viewer's experiences. The unpredictability of diluted paints adds an external input to my process, which I refine, enhance, or leave untouched to create a layered narrative. Inspired by Euclidean geometry, I challenge conventional logic with suspended objects and irrational shadows, inviting the viewer to decide on the most appropriate perspective.
