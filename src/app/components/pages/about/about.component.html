About me

Where I come from

I was born in Radom, Poland, and grew up in Tarnobrzeg. After earning my degree from Wroclaw University of Economics in 2011, I continued to expand my education and artistic horizons. In 2019, I graduated from the Eugeniusz Geppert Academy of Fine Arts and Design in Wroclaw, where I studied painting under the tutelage of Prof. Piotr Błażejewski and Dr. Anna Kołodziejczyk.

Statement

I have chosen visual arts and painting specifically because I am fascinated by visual language and its flexibility. Over the years, I developed an abstract style that balances organic forms and geometry. I explore how man-made objects and nature influence people. I talk about an intimacy between a user, observer, tourist, bus driver, and inanimate objects. That interaction is subconscious on a daily basis, yet it still leaves a trace in our daily routines, the way we spend time, and influences our mood (inspired by "The Architecture of Happiness" by Alain de Botton).

I think of a painting as a message that can be read and interpreted—the more specific the sending message is, the easier it is to decipher it. Each element of visual language, such as color, composition, and shape, plays its role. When I start working on a painting, I only have a rough idea of how it should look. I use diluted paints, and depending on the thickness of the solution, I have limited control over the actual flow of the medium. I consider that element of unpredictability an external input and later decide which parts should be painted over, refined, or enhanced. I use masking tape or silicone to create sharp edges. In most cases, I leave some parts of the initial layer untouched so that all the steps remain visible.

Inspired by architecture, I use perspective to stack forms in a logical order and then disrupt it with an irrational shadow or an object that seems suspended in air, leaving the viewer to decide which order is the most appropriate. What seems to be obviously wrong in Euclidean geometry might be true under different circumstances.

Although I tend to draw inspiration from specific objects and landscapes, I try to focus on the feelings they evoke, bearing in mind that there is no universal "this is how I feel about it now." What I try to communicate using a set of available tools can be interpreted differently by a receiver with their own unique experiences. That freedom and self-awareness are mixed with confusion and uncertainty.

The process

Allow me to walk you through my painting process

The creation of a painting is a journey of transformation and expression, where an initial spark of inspiration evolves through various stages into a captivating piece of art. Each step, from the selection of colors to the meticulous layering, plays a vital role in bringing the artist's vision to life, crafting a visual narrative that speaks both to the creator and the audience.

Inspiration

The journey begins with a spark of inspiration, where ideas and emotions blend to form the concept of the artwork

Color Selection

This stage involves carefully choosing a palette, where each color is selected for its ability to convey the intended mood and atmosphere.

Shape Outlining

Here, the initial outlines and shapes are drawn, laying the foundation for the painting's structure and composition.

Layers

This crucial phase involves applying layers of paint, each adding depth, texture, and richness to the composition, enhancing the visual impact of the artwork.

Finishing touches

In the final stage, detailed elements and refinements are added, bringing the painting to life with its full depth and vibrancy.
